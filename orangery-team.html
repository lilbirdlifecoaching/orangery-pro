The offerings

A simple set of options for leaders who need help with the whole room.

These can stand alone or combine. The aim is practical movement in communication, trust, conflict, and shared leadership maturity.

01 · Team coaching

Intact team coaching

For leadership teams or working groups who need a steadier rhythm of reflection, clarity, and follow-through together.

  • Shared language for how the team currently operates
  • Live work on trust, ownership, and hard conversations
  • A coaching cadence that builds maturity between meetings
02 · Workshops

Communication and dynamics workshops

Focused sessions that help people understand wiring, reduce friction, and communicate with more intention under pressure.

  • 5 Voices and GiANT tools applied to real team dynamics
  • Practical work on feedback, conflict, and influence
  • Useful for offsites, leadership days, or reset moments
03 · In-team coach

Train an in-team coach

Build capacity inside the organisation so someone can hold lighter coaching and facilitation between formal sessions.

  • A defined training block using GiANT and 5 Voices language
  • Light as-needed support via Slack, messages, and a monthly call
  • Stronger continuity without needing outside support for every moment
04 · Exec / board / org

Exec, board, and company-wide support

For senior rooms or broader organisational pathways where the need is larger than a single team package.

  • Facilitation for alignment and decision-making conversations
  • Support around tension, trust, and leadership presence
  • Custom scoping through conversation rather than self-quote

Who this is for

Built for organisations that feel the cost of unclear communication and messy people dynamics.

If individual coaching is helping one leader, but the system around them is still creating friction, this is usually the better door.

A strong fit

orangery.team tends to land well when:

  • A leadership team needs clearer ownership and healthier challenge
  • Communication has become either too careful or too blunt
  • An exec group or board wants better alignment without theatrics
  • You want practical development, not generic culture slogans

Probably better elsewhere

This may not be the right stream if:

  • You primarily need one-to-one coaching for a single leader — start with orangery.pro
  • You are looking for pure consulting strategy with no relational work
  • You want a one-off motivational talk with no follow-through
